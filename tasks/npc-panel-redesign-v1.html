What changed

  • Actions: 6 buttons in 3 rows → 1 row. Only + NPC stays a full button (the thing you do most). Library, Animal, Select, and Show/Hide-all collapse to small icons. Show-all / Hide-all live behind the eye icon (or a tiny menu) instead of two loud bars.
  • The per-group Show/Hide bars are gone. Each group now carries one small eye on its header row — green = shown on the map, red/muted = hidden. Click it to toggle. That single change removes most of the color noise.
  • Filters stay but tighten to one wrapped row, with a thin divider splitting disposition (Friendly/Goon/Foe/Antagonist) from status (Active/Dead) so the two axes read as separate.
  • Group rows are now light dividers, not boxed cards — denser, easier to scan a long list, and the count sits quietly on the right.
