Stress Check (mid-play CHECK button + at-max auto-fire, locked 2026-05-19)

Two modes share the same <RollModal> shell. Mid-play fires from the manual CHECK button on the stress bar (GM-called, success = no change, failure = +1 stress, cascades into at-max if +1 fills the bar). At-max fires when stress hits 5 (or via the mid-play cascade) — success drops to 4, failure rolls Breaking Point. Label suffix (at max) distinguishes the two on the saved row. Mechanical bits (+1 stress / drop to 4) live in the expanded view + the stress pips updating live; never in the compact narrative.

Failure = nothing. Dire Failure = -1 WP immediate (visible on target's HP pips, not in narrative). Low Insight = target makes Wound Infection check (auto-cascade fires on the patient's client).
