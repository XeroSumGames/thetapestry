### About (3 paragraphs - replace all 3 placeholders, all begin with "[PLACEHOLDER paragraph N]")

**Paragraph 1 (what it is):**
> The Tapestry is a virtual tabletop built specifically for Distemper, an independent tabletop RPG set in a post-pandemic shared world. Unlike general-purpose VTTs, Tapestry ships every system Distemper needs already wired: character creation, encoded mechanics, an interactive tactical map, real-time chat, vehicle and community systems, and modular adventures called Rumors that any GM can publish or remix. The platform enforces canon at the code level, so the rules at your table are the rules every group is playing.

**Paragraph 2 (setting + audience):**
> The DistemperVerse is a post-pandemic North America fifteen years after the world stopped working. Players take the role of Survivors and Thrivers; GMs run campaigns that span weeks or years and link into a wider shared canon. Settlements rise and fall. People remember. The setting is its own thing - not zombies, not fallout, not the next generic survival game. It is a world where small communities scratch out a living against the question of who gets to stay, who gets to leave, and who gets remembered.

**Paragraph 3 (status + invitation):**
> Tapestry is in public beta as of 2026 with an active playtester group and limited invite access. A Kickstarter campaign opens 2026-09-01 to fund the next year of development - the TTRPG core books, the supplemental setting modules, and the platform's path from beta to v1.0. Reviewers, YouTubers, podcasters, and bloggers are welcome and supported. Press inquiries via the contact above; review copies and demo access available on request.
