# Space: 1999 Character Generator - eyeball test plan (2026-07-10)

Open the generator I just rendered in chat (or open
`D:\coding\space1999generator\index.html` in a browser). Do these in order and
report back what you saw.

1. Click the **Randomise** button (top right of the header). Watch it jump
   straight to the Dossier with a fully filled Alphan. Read it over.
2. Click **Start Over** (bottom of the dossier). You should land back on Step 1.
3. On Step 1 (Concept), click a few department cards. Click **Security**, then
   press **Continue**.
4. On Step 2 (Skills & Attitudes): in the Skills panel, click the **6** button
   on one skill and the **5** button on a different skill. Use the **+ / -**
   steppers to spend the 3 bonus points. Then in the Attitudes panel, use the
   steppers until the "left" counter reads 0. Press **Continue**.
5. On Step 3 (Focuses & Talents): tick 4 focuses (at least one marked with an
   orange diamond) and 3 talents. If a talent shows "(skill)" or "(attitude)",
   a dropdown appears - pick something in it. Press **Continue**.
6. On Step 4 (Finishing): pick a Hope from the dropdown, type or pick two
   Traits, type a Signature Item, and type a Name. Press **Continue**.
7. On Step 5 (Dossier): read the whole sheet top to bottom. Click
   **Print Dossier** and check the print preview looks clean, then cancel it.
8. Try clicking the small dice (Roll) buttons on individual panels (Skills,
   Attitudes, Focuses, Talents, Hope, Traits, Name) and watch them fill just
   that section.
9. Resize the browser narrow (or open on your phone) and confirm nothing runs
   off the side of the screen.

Report back: anything that looked wrong, confusing, ugly, or that you'd want
worded differently. I'll parse it against the rules and fix.
