## Setup
Have 3-5 image files on your desktop (any JPGs or PNGs). You'll also need an NPC in any campaign.

---

## Part 1 - Tab visibility

1. Go to `thetapestry.distemperverse.com/tools/token-creator` while logged in as your Thriver account.
2. Report what tabs you see at the top of the page and which one is active by default.

---

## Part 2 - Drop zone and preview

3. On the Bulk Upload tab, click the drop zone and select 3 image files.
4. Wait a few seconds for the preview grid to load.
5. Report what the grid looks like - how many cards, whether each has a circular thumbnail, a name field, and three buttons labeled M / F / -.

---

## Part 3 - Name editing and gender toggle

6. Click into the name field on the first card and change the text to "Test Portrait One".
7. Click the **M** button on the first card.
8. Click the **F** button on the second card.
9. Leave the third card on **-**.
10. Report what the three cards show in their name fields and which gender button is highlighted on each.

---

## Part 4 - Upload

11. Click **Upload All**.
12. Wait for all three cards to finish (watch the status text under each card).
13. Report the status shown on each card when it stops changing.

---

## Part 5 - Portrait picker (NPC)

14. Open any campaign, go to the NPC roster, open any NPC, click the portrait picker button.
15. In the picker, click the **All** tab.
16. Report whether you can see any of the three portraits you just uploaded.
17. Click the **Male** tab. Report whether "Test Portrait One" (the M-tagged one) is visible.
18. Click the **Female** tab. Report whether the F-tagged portrait is visible.
19. Check whether the **-** (Other) portrait appears under the Male or Female tab.

---

## Part 6 - Thriver Crop & Upload regression

20. Click the **Crop & Upload** tab.
21. Drop one image onto the drop zone.
22. Report whether the circle editor appears and the Download button is available.

---

Report back what you saw for each numbered step.
