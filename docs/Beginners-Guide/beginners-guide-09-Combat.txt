====================================================================
 9. COMBAT
====================================================================

Combat is a structured mode you enter from the Table during an active session. The GM starts and ends it.


STARTING COMBAT (GM)
--------------------
1. Click "Start Combat" in the Table header.
2. Pick which NPCs are in this fight.
3. Optionally choose a "Drop" character - they act first, alone, before the rest of the round.
4. Every combatant rolls initiative: 2d6 + Acumen + Dexterity.
5. The initiative bar appears below the header, sorted highest to lowest.
6. The tactical map auto-shares to players.

[screenshot: initiative bar with portraits + numbers]


THE INITIATIVE BAR
------------------
Shows everyone in turn order: portrait, name, initiative roll, action pips, and status icons. The active combatant wears a gold ring and turn arrow on the tactical map. The round counter sits next to the bar.

GM-only controls per combatant: Defer, Grant, Skip, Remove. Use Defer to push a turn down; use Grant to hand a turn to someone else.

Hidden combatants render as a HIDDEN chip with a Reveal button (eye icon) for the GM. Players see "Waiting..." during a hidden combatant's turn.


TURNS AND ROUNDS
----------------
Every combatant gets 2 ACTIONS per turn. The active combatant's action bar appears below the initiative order. When both actions are spent (or the GM clicks Next Turn), play moves to the next combatant.

When everyone has acted, a new round begins. Initiative re-rolls and everyone gets 2 fresh actions.


COMBAT ACTIONS
--------------
Most actions cost 1; exceptions noted.

  AIM                 +2 CMod on your next attack. Lost if you don't attack next.
  ATTACK              Roll your equipped weapon. Pick a target from the dropdown. Damage applies on a hit.
  CHARGE (2)          Move up to 20 ft, then make a melee or unarmed attack.
  COORDINATE          Roll Tactics. Allies within Close range get +2 CMod against a chosen target.
  DEFEND              +2 defensive bonus against the next attack.
  GRAPPLE             Opposed PHY + Unarmed check on an Engaged target. See "Grappling" below.
  MOVE                Highlight cells within 9 ft. Click to move.
  RAPID FIRE (2)      Two shots: -1 CMod, then -3 CMod.
  READY WEAPON        Switch, Reload, or Unjam / Repair via a sub-modal.
  REPOSITION          Free end-of-round positioning.
  SPRINT (2)          Move 30 ft (3x normal). Requires Athletics check.
  SUBDUE              Opposed PHY + Unarmed choke against a target you have grappled. See "Grappling" below.
  TAKE COVER          +2 defense for ALL attacks this round. Once per round.
  UNARMED             Physicality + Unarmed Combat, 1d3 damage. Always available.
  STABILIZE           Roll Medicine to stop a mortally wounded ally's death countdown. Must be within 5 ft.


GRAPPLING
---------
Grappling is an opposed PHY + Unarmed (or Athletics) check, run through the dedicated grapple modal. Three flows live in the same modal:

  GRAPPLE     The grappler attempts the hold. On success, the target is grappled and loses an action. If they have actions left this turn, one is consumed now; if not, the loss carries to next turn.

  SUBDUE      The grappler (with a target already in hand) attempts a choke. Opposed check again. On a hold, the grappled target takes 1 WP plus (1d3 + PHY AMod + Unarmed SMod) RP.

  BREAK FREE  The grappled defender (it's their turn) spends an action to escape. The grappler contests reactively, free of charge. On success, the defender is free. On failure or tie, the defender takes 1 RP and the hold persists.

While grappled, every attack against the grappled target gains +1 CMod automatically; it shows in the CMod breakdown.

Any successful hit against the grappler (a stray shot, an ally's attack) breaks the hold. The target is released and a log row records the strike-and-release.

Insight Dice can be spent on either side at any step.


BLAST RADIUS
------------
Grenades, RPGs, and Molotovs apply damage automatically:

  Engaged (5 ft)   full damage
  Close (30 ft)    50% damage
  Far (100 ft)     25% damage

The attacker is excluded from splash. Throwing one decrements the stack from your inventory (these aren't tied to a clip).


MORTAL WOUNDS
-------------
When a character's WP hits 0:
  - A death countdown starts (4 + Physicality rounds).
  - If the character has any Insight Dice, a modal asks whether to trade ALL of them to survive with 1 WP and 1 RP. Only the player who owns the character can make that choice.
  - If nobody stabilizes them before the countdown expires, the character dies.

Whenever a PC enters WP=0 or RP=0, even if they're saved, they gain 1 Stress.


LASTING WOUNDS
--------------
A PC who survives a mortal wound rolls Physicality. On a success, no Lasting Wound. On a failure, the system opens the Lasting Wound modal: 2d6 against Table 12 returns a permanent injury (Broken Rib, Mangled Hand, Concussion, etc.) with its mechanical penalty.

Lasting Wounds appear as red chips on the character card. They heal slowly through Rest and Medicine, and faster through Evolution. The character card "Show / Hide" toggles whether players see each other's lasting wounds.


INFECTION
---------
Distemper-era wounds get infected. When you end combat, the system fires a Wound Infection check for every wounded combatant - the roll modal opens automatically and walks each one through. The result either clears the infection or escalates the wound.

A wound-infection-warning banner appears on the first wound of each combat as a reminder.

Heal rolls that come up Low Insight auto-fire a Wound Infection check on the patient. Healing badly can make things worse.

Sickness from environmental sources (bad water, exposure, contagion) follows the same ladder. A Dire Failure can escalate Sickness to Mortally Wounded.


WEAPON MALFUNCTION
------------------
An attack roll that comes up Low Insight (1+1) drops a weapon-malfunction log row. The weapon's condition drops a level (Pristine to Used, Used to Worn, etc.). Down at Damaged or Broken, it needs Unjam / Repair before it can be used again.


ENVIRONMENTAL DAMAGE
--------------------
The "Env Dmg" button on the character card handles Falling and Drowning damage outside the combat-action flow.

  FALLING   3 WP and 3 RP per 10 feet fallen.
  DROWNING  3 WP and 3 RP per round past your hold-breath limit (6 + PHY AMod rounds).

Click the button, pick Falling or Drowning, enter the distance or rounds, and the damage applies with a log row.


TRAVEL PUSH
-----------
The "Travel" button on the character card applies travel-time costs.

Up to 8 hours of contiguous travel is free. Past that, each additional hour costs 1 RP. The button advances the campaign clock by the hours you enter and writes the cost to the feed.


REST AND RECOVERY
-----------------
The "Rest" button on the character card opens a rest modal.

  - Pick Hours, Days, or Weeks of rest.
  - WP heals at +1 per day. A PC still recovering from a mortal wound heals at half that rate - automatic, no toggle.
  - RP recovers at +1 per hour. If the character is sick, recovery caps at half-max - automatic.
  - For 8 or more hours, a checkbox appears: "Uninterrupted and enjoyable (free from threat)." Tick it to drop 1 Stress per 8 hours.

Click "Apply Healing." The clock advances, stats update, the feed gets a single summary row.


ENDING COMBAT (GM)
------------------
Click "End Combat" in the header. Initiative clears. Grapple states release. Mortally wounded PCs gain +1 Stress.

End Combat fires the post-combat Wound Infection roll modal for every wounded combatant.
