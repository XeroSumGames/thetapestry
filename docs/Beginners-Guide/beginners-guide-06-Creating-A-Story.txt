====================================================================
 6. CREATING A STORY
====================================================================

A STORY is your campaign - the whole ongoing thing. Inside one Story you can run encounters (single fights or scenes), adventures (multi-session arcs), or full campaigns (long-running storylines). The Story is the only formal container; you organize the content inside it however you like.


CREATING ONE (GM)
-----------------
Click "My Stories" in the sidebar, then "+ New Story." Fill in:

  - Name (required).
  - Description (optional).
  - Setting - pick one:
      Custom Setting     start blank, no pre-seeded content.
      District Zero      pre-loaded with the District Zero sourcebook NPCs, locations, and pins.
      Kings Crossroads   the Delaware setting, a small strip-mall community in Sussex County.
  - Map style and starting location (the campaign map's default view).
  - OR - start from a Rumor (a published module) instead of a setting. See Chapter 14. Older packaged settings (Mongrels, The Arena, and others) live in the Rumors marketplace as modules; pick them from there.

You become this Story's GM automatically.

When created, the Story gets an INVITE CODE / LINK. Share it with your players. See "Joining" below.


THE STORY PAGE
--------------
The GM action bar sits at the top of the Story page:

  Launch      opens the Table (Chapter 7) in a new tab.
  GM Notes    opens the GM Notes popout in its own window - story overview, scenes, NPC list, plot beats. Docks nicely on a second monitor.
  Snapshot    view or roll back to a saved campaign state. New snapshots are also captured on demand from the Table's GM Tools dropdown.
  Publish     publishes this Story's content as a Rumor module. Once published, this button re-labels to "Module vX.Y.Z" and shows the current version. See Chapter 14.
  Archive     appears once you've published a module. Archives the module (pulls it from the marketplace, notifies subscribers) while keeping the Story itself intact.
  Delete      permanent. Asks you to type the Story's name to confirm so a misclick can't end a campaign.
  GM Kit      packages every pin, NPC, scene, token, and handout (with images) into a portable .zip. Handy for offline reference or backups.

An "Update available" purple button appears in-line when your Story is subscribed to a Rumor module that has a newer version - click through to the version history to review and pull the update.

Below the action bar the Story page lists your player roster, pending pregens (if the setting ships with them), and inline GM Tools for editing name, description, map style, and starting location.

JOINING A STORY (PLAYER)
------------------------
The GM gives you an invite code or link. Two ways to use it:

  - Click the link. The "Join" page opens. Pick which of your characters to bring. Confirm.
  - Or go to "Join a Story" and paste the code.

You appear on the GM's player roster immediately and a notification fires for the GM.


LEAVING
-------
On your "My Stories" page, click "Leave" on any Story you're a player in. (GMs can't leave their own Story; they delete it instead.)
