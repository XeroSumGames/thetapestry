====================================================================
 6. CREATING A STORY
====================================================================

A STORY is your campaign - the whole ongoing thing. Inside one Story you can run encounters (single fights or scenes), adventures (multi-session arcs), or full campaigns (long-running storylines). The Story is the only formal container; you organize the content inside it however you like.


CREATING ONE (GM)
-----------------
Click "My Stories" in the sidebar, then "+ New Story." Fill in:

  - Name (required).
  - Description (optional).
  - Setting - pick one:
      Custom         start blank.
      District Zero  pre-loaded with the District Zero sourcebook NPCs, locations, and pins.
      Chased         the Delaware setting from the Chased magazine.
      Mongrels       the canonical demo campaign.
      The Arena      the gladiator sandbox.
      Empty          truly blank, no seed content.
  - Map style and starting location (the campaign map's default view).
  - OR - start from a Rumor (a published module). See Chapter 14.

You become this Story's GM automatically.

When created, the Story gets an INVITE CODE / LINK. Share it with your players. See "Joining" below.


THE STORY PAGE
--------------
Lists everything about your Story:

  Launch        opens the Table (Chapter 7).
  Edit          inline form on the page itself - name, description, map style, map center.
  Clone         copy the entire Story as a new one.
  Share         copies the invite link to your clipboard.
  Pregens       drop-in pre-built characters seeded from the chosen setting.
  Sessions      full session history.
  Snapshots     saved states of the campaign you can roll back to.
  Publish       publish this Story's content as a Rumor module (Chapter 14).
  Delete        permanent, asks first.

The GM Tools area is inline on the page now - no separate edit page to chase.

JOINING A STORY (PLAYER)
------------------------
The GM gives you an invite code or link. Two ways to use it:

  - Click the link. The "Join" page opens. Pick which of your characters to bring. Confirm.
  - Or go to "Join a Story" and paste the code.

You appear on the GM's player roster immediately and a notification fires for the GM.


LEAVING
-------
On your "My Stories" page, click "Leave" on any Story you're a player in. (GMs can't leave their own Story; they delete it instead.)
