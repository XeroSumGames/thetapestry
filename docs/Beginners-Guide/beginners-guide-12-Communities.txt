====================================================================
 12. COMMUNITIES
====================================================================

A COMMUNITY is a group of PCs and NPCs bound together as a household, settlement, band, or enclave. Communities are one of the flagship Distemper mechanics - they turn the people your party knows into a roster with morale, resources, roles, and weekly checks that shape the fiction between adventures.

The full rules are in The Rules / Communities. The Tapestry implements them mechanically; this chapter walks through the platform.


GROUP vs COMMUNITY - THE 13-MEMBER THRESHOLD
--------------------------------------------
Until a group has 13 combined members (PCs + NPCs), it's a GROUP. Groups don't run weekly checks.

At 13+ members it becomes a COMMUNITY and weekly mechanics unlock:
  - Weekly Fed / Clothed / Morale checks.
  - Role minimums for the labor pool.
  - A leader who rolls Morale.
  - Failure states: 25% / 50% / 75% departures, dissolution after three consecutive failures.

If a Community drops back below 13 it reverts to a Group; weekly checks pause until it grows again.

A status chip on the community header shows the state: green Community, amber Group, red Dissolved.


CREATING A COMMUNITY (GM)
-------------------------
On the Table, click Community dropdown, then "New Community." Fill in:

  - Name (required).
  - Description (optional).
  - Homestead pin (optional). Pick an existing campaign pin - the community is narratively rooted there.

You're auto-enrolled as the founder and the default Leader.


ADDING AND REMOVING MEMBERS
---------------------------
Inside an expanded community, the "Add Member" form (GM only) adds NPCs from your campaign roster or PCs from your player list. Pick:

  - Subject kind (NPC or PC).
  - The specific person.
  - Their role.
  - Their recruitment type (Member, Cohort, Conscript, Convert, Founder).

The mechanical way to bring NPCs in during play is the Recruitment Check (Chapter 10).

Remove a member with the X button on their row. Members are soft-removed with a reason: morale-departure, dissolution, manual, or killed.


ROLES (NPCs ONLY)
-----------------
Every NPC member has one of five roles. PCs aren't counted toward labor minimums.

  GATHERER     Hunting, foraging, farming, scavenging. Rolls the weekly Fed Check.
  MAINTAINER   Repair, clothing, supplies, tools. Rolls the weekly Clothed Check.
  SAFETY       Patrols and emergency services. Modifies Morale.
  UNASSIGNED   Idle. Pulls down the labor pool if anything's understaffed.
  ASSIGNED     Off doing a PC-directed mission. Carries a directing PC and a task description.


ROLE MINIMUMS
-------------
The NPC labor pool (everyone whose role is not Assigned) has minimum fractions:

  GATHERERS   at least 33%
  MAINTAINERS at least 20%
  SAFETY      5-10% (at least 1 if the pool is tiny)

The role bars on the community body show current vs minimum counts. Green means OK; red means understaffed. The "Re-balance Roles" button auto-assigns NPCs to roles based on their skills.


THE LEADER
----------
One PC or NPC rolls the weekly Morale Check on the community's behalf. The founder is leader by default.

Setting the leader: in the community body, the Leader row has a dropdown listing every member.

Stepping down: the GM (always) and the current PC leader (themselves) can abdicate. A successor picker opens with auto-pick, leave-leaderless, and specific-member options. Leaving leaderless costs -1 on Morale Checks.


THE WEEKLY CHECK
----------------
Once per in-game week, the GM clicks "Run Weekly Check" on the community body. A modal fires three rolls in sequence:

  1. FED CHECK     Gatherers roll for Rations.
  2. CLOTHED CHECK Maintainers roll for Supplies.
  3. MORALE CHECK  The Leader rolls.

Fed and Clothed feed directly into the Morale modifier slots.


THE MORALE MODIFIER SLOTS
-------------------------
Six built-in slots plus a freeform GM slot:

  1. FED                 Fed Check outcome.
  2. CLOTHED             Clothed Check outcome.
  3. MOOD AROUND THE     Last week's Morale carry-over.
     CAMPFIRE
  4. ENOUGH HANDS        Role minimums.
  5. A CLEAR VOICE       Leader presence.
  6. SOMEONE TO WATCH    Safety coverage.
     OVER ME
  7. ADDITIONAL          GM freeform - raids, miracles, weather.

Hover any slot label to read the SRD rule text in a tooltip. The GM can override any slot before rolling.


MORALE OUTCOMES
---------------
    Roll        Outcome          Departures   Next Mood
    -------------------------------------------------
    6+6         High Insight       none          +2
    14+         Wild Success       none          +1
    9-13        Success            none           0
    4-8         Failure            25%           -1
    0-3         Dire Failure       50%           -2
    1+1         Low Insight        75%           -3

Departures soft-remove NPCs. Removal priority:
    Unassigned -> Cohort -> Convert -> Conscript -> Founder -> Apprentice

Apprentices and Founders stay the longest. Any success resets the consecutive-failure counter; any failure increments it.


DISSOLUTION
-----------
Three failed Morale Checks in a row collapses the community. At 2 / 3 the panel shows a red warning.

If the third failure hits, the result panel offers a Retention Check - one immediate Morale Check using only the Mood slot. Success at any tier saves the community (consecutive failures drop to 2, one week of cushion). Failure proceeds with dissolution.

Dissolution is permanent. Make a new community if you want a similar story.


SKIP WEEK
---------
"Skip Week" bumps the week counter without rolling. Mood carries over from the most recently rolled check.


THE COMMUNITY DASHBOARD (GM)
----------------------------
Full-screen view reachable from the Community dropdown. Per community:
  - Header: status, week, consecutive failures, member count.
  - Morale History (last 20 weeks).
  - Resource History (last 40 Fed / Clothed rolls).
  - Current Role Distribution (bars vs minimums).
  - Recruitment Stats (success rate by approach).
  - Members by Recruitment Type.


THE AT-A-GLANCE BLOCK (PLAYER VIEW)
-----------------------------------
At the top of every expanded community panel, a small block summarizes the community for the viewer:

  RECENT MORALE
    Five colored letter chips (W / H / S / F / D / L) for the last five weekly outcomes.

  YOU
    Your role. Founder tag if applicable. Your Apprentice (if any). A list of NPCs you personally recruited.

Everyone sees this.


THE COMMUNITY FEED
------------------
Each community has its own activity feed accessible from the Campfire (Chapter 13). The feed shows community-scoped posts - recruitments, big morale events, dissolutions, narrative happenings. Players and the GM can post directly into a community's feed.
