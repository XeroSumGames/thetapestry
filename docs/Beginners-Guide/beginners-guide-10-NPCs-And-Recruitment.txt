====================================================================
 10. NPCs AND RECRUITMENT
====================================================================

NPCs are everyone in the world who isn't a PC. They're managed in the right-panel NPCs tab on the Table.


CREATING AN NPC (GM)
--------------------
Click "+ Add NPC" to open the modal:

  - Name.
  - Type: Friendly, Goon, Foe, or Antagonist. Sets baseline RAPID values.
  - Portrait: pick from the Portrait Bank, upload a new image, or pick a tier-colored silhouette as a placeholder.
  - RAPID attributes (adjustable).
  - Skills (add from dropdown, set level 0-4).
  - Weapon (primary, all categories).
  - Secondary Weapon (Foe and Antagonist only).
  - Motivation and Complication (pick or roll).
  - Three personality words.
  - GM Notes (private, never shown to players).
  - Status: Active, Dead, or Unknown.


GENERATING AN NPC
-----------------
Click "Generate NPC" and pick a type. The system rolls a random NPC with stats, skills, weapons, personality, and a portrait pulled from the public Portrait Bank.


POPULATING A SCENE (GM)
-----------------------
The "Populate" tool bulk-generates a roster along the SRD's recommended distribution: 1 Antagonist, 2 Foes, 3 Goons, 4 Friendlies (the 1A:2F:3G:4B triangle).

Populate pulls portraits from the public Portrait Bank, cycling through unused entries by gender. If the Bank runs short, the unfilled slots come out portrait-less; you can fill them by hand or via the silhouette placeholders.


THE PORTRAIT BANK AND TOKEN CREATOR
-----------------------------------
Portraits and PC tokens share one shared library: the Portrait Bank. Players can browse and contribute through the Token Creator at /tools/token-creator.

  BULK UPLOAD tab (anyone signed in)
    Drop in multiple images at once. Each image gets a card with three buttons: M, F, and - (untagged).
      M / F  Tag the portrait by gender. The system auto-numbers it (e.g. "NPC-MAN-001") and files it in the public bank for random NPC assignment.
      -      Untagged. The portrait is saved privately under your own name. It won't appear in anyone else's campaign or any random NPC assignment.
    A "Private" checkbox on each card forces public uploads out of random assignment.
    The folder picker loads every image from a chosen folder at once - convenient when you have a stash to add.

  CROP & UPLOAD tab (Admins only)
    Drop a single image, drag a circle to crop, set quality, pick a gender, click Download. Uploads cropped versions at three sizes plus stores the original in the bank.


CLONING
-------
Click the + button on any NPC card. The clone gets an auto-numbered name ("Kincaid's Soldier #2") and appears just below the original.


THE NPC TAB
-----------
NPC cards live in folders. Drag and drop reorders them within a folder. Players can reorder their own folder view independently.

Each card shows:
  Drag handle, Delete (X), Clone (+), name, status badge, Show / Hide button, Fight button (combat only), Map button (place / remove a token), portrait, Lasting Wounds chip (if any).

Click the card to open the full NPC sheet:
  - RAPID attributes (clickable for rolls during sessions).
  - WP / RP dot trackers.
  - Skills (clickable for rolls).
  - Unarmed attack button.
  - Weapon attack button.
  - Equipment attacks.
  - Restore button (if mortally wounded or dead - resets to full HP).
  - Recruit button (during an active session).
  - Edit / Close buttons.

"Close All" closes every open NPC sheet. Esc does the same.


REVEALING NPCs TO PLAYERS
-------------------------
Click "Show" on an NPC card to reveal them. Players see name, portrait, and type. Click "Hide" to remove visibility.

When combat starts, hidden NPCs auto-flip to revealed so their initiative entries aren't anonymous. To keep an NPC hidden through combat (an ambush, a watcher), use the per-combatant Hidden chip on the initiative bar (Chapter 9). Players see "Waiting..." during a Hidden combatant's turn.

A panic-hide button is available on the NPC tab header. One click hides every revealed NPC across every folder. Useful when a player walks into the room you weren't expecting.


PUBLISHING TO THE WORLD LIBRARY
-------------------------------
From the NPC edit modal, click "Publish to World Library." Other GMs can import your published NPCs into their Stories.


RECRUITMENT
-----------
Recruitment is the mechanical way PCs bring NPCs into a Community during play. Open the Recruit modal three ways:

  - NPC card "Recruit" button (active session only).
  - Community menu / Recruit.
  - Checks dropdown / Recruit.

The modal walks pick (approach + skill), roll, and result. The roll:

    2d6 + Influence + chosen skill + CMod (First Impression + other situational mods)


APPROACHES
----------
  COHORT     Shared interest or goal. NPC joins until the next Morale Check, then re-evaluates.
  CONSCRIPT  Coerced service. Requires a credible threat - weapons drawn, leverage held, escape cut off.
  CONVERT    Shared belief, ideology, or vision. Joins as probationary; commits fully after surviving the next Morale Check.

Some NPCs lock certain approaches. A Convert won't be Conscripted, a Conscript won't Cohort with you, and so on. The modal grays out unavailable approaches per NPC. Hover the locked tile for the reason.


APPROACH-SPECIFIC OUTCOMES
--------------------------
Each approach has its own Success and Failure narrative. A failed Cohort doesn't read the same as a failed Conscript. The roll feed surfaces the right banner automatically.

A Conscripted NPC starts with a hidden Morale counter that drains every weekly check. If their counter hits zero, the GM gets an "Escape Pending" surface warning them the NPC is about to bolt. Either re-roll Conscription, change approach, or accept the escape.


APPRENTICES (FOURTH OPTION)
---------------------------
A fourth recruitment type, APPRENTICE, unlocks ONLY on a Moment of High Insight (double-6) recruitment roll. Wild Success (plain 14+) doesn't count. One Apprentice per PC, ever.

The Apprentice arrives with a name, age, randomly rolled Motivation and Complication, three descriptive words, a setting-appropriate Profession, and 1 CDP in each vocational skill. Their attributes start with 3 unspent CDP and their skills have 5 unspent CDP.

The PC Fills In The Gaps about the Apprentice's background. Over one in-game month the PC can train the Apprentice in any one skill up to (PC skill level - 1). Future CDP the PC earns can be spent on the Apprentice instead.

The Apprentice bond survives Morale-driven departures. They stay with you unless you die, dismiss them, or they're killed.

In the community roster, Apprentice rows show a bond line:
    JESS BROOKER <- ADA MAREN    [Unassigned]  X
      Task: Training with Ada in the garage    edit


PRESSGANGING - THE CREDIBLE-THREAT GATE
---------------------------------------
The SRD says: "The PCs must present a credible threat for Conscription to work."

When you pick Conscript, a red banner appears under the approach tiles. When you click Roll, a blocking confirm asks you to acknowledge the threat is real. Cancel = no roll, no resources burned. Cohort and Convert never show this gate.
