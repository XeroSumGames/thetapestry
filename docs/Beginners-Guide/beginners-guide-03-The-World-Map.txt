====================================================================
 3. THE WORLD MAP
====================================================================

Two maps exist in The Tapestry: the global WORLD MAP, and each Story's CAMPAIGN MAP. They use the same engine and most of the same controls.


THE WORLD MAP
-------------
Click "The World" in the sidebar. You'll see an interactive map centered on North America - that's the world of Distemper.

CONTROLS
  - Pan: click and drag.
  - Zoom: scroll wheel, or the +/- buttons.
  - Search: top-right - type a place or address, click a suggestion to fly there.
  - Map style: top-right - Satellite, Topo, Street, Voyager, Humanitarian, Positron, or Dark.

[screenshot: map with style toggle highlighted]

PINS PANEL (right side, toggle with "Pins")
  PUBLIC tab    Every approved world pin, organized into folders by category. Click a folder to expand. Click a pin to fly to it and see its notes and images.
  MY PINS tab   Pins you placed. Edit, delete, or toggle public/private.
  CAMPAIGN tab  Pins from any Story you're in that the GM has revealed.
  WHISPERS tab  A public message wall. Drop a short note for anyone to read. Distinct from in-Story chat and open to everyone.

The eye icon next to each category folder hides or shows that whole category on the map. The search box at the top of the panel filters pins by name.


PLACING A PIN
-------------
Click anywhere on the empty map. A form opens. Fill in the title, notes, and one or more categories. Optionally upload images. Save.

As a Survivor, your pin goes to the moderation queue and shows as a Rumor (?) until reviewed. As an Admin, it goes live immediately.


THE CAMPAIGN MAP
----------------
Each Story has its own map, accessible from the Table (Chapter 7). The campaign map has the same controls as the world map, but with extra GM tools:

  - Click the empty map to place a campaign pin (instant, no moderation).
  - Drag pins to reposition.
  - Show / Hide a pin to control whether players see it.
  - Link a pin to a tactical scene - players can then double-click the pin to open that scene (Chapter 8).
  - Promote a pin to the world map (sends it to moderation for global visibility).

Players see only revealed pins. Click any pin to read its notes and view its images.


MEASURING AND ROUTES
--------------------
Two tools in the map toolbar handle distance:

  📏 MEASURE  Click two or more points on the map. A straight-line ruler connects them and shows total distance. Esc clears.

  🛣 ROUTE   Click to enter Route mode. The status line prompts "Click your starting point..." Click your start, click each waypoint (or Alt-click a pin to snap to it), then click a destination. The planner draws the real road route through every point using OpenStreetMap data and shows total distance plus an estimated driving time based on the selected travel mode. Esc clears.

Both tools are local to your view - other players don't see your measurement or route unless you push it with Share.


VEHICLE BUBBLE
--------------
When a vehicle is in play (Chapter 11), a small bubble floats on the campaign map at the party's current position. It shows the vehicle name and clicks through to the vehicle's sheet.
