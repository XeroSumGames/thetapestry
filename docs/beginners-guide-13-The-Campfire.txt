====================================================================
 13. THE CAMPFIRE
====================================================================

The Campfire is the cross-Story community hub. Click "The Campfire" in the sidebar to land on it.

The Campfire is a PORTAL: cards on the landing page, drill-down via tabs. Click a card to open that tab; the URL updates so you can deep-link back.


THE PORTAL
----------
Three sections render on the landing page:

  SETTING HUBS
    District Zero and Kings Crossroads each have their own hub. A hub is a setting-specific feed: canon timeline pins, published communities tied to that setting, and recent posts from Forums, War Stories, and LFG filtered to the setting.

  FEATURED MODULE
    A single hero card highlighting a featured Rumor from the marketplace. Links into /rumors. See Chapter 14.

  EXPLORE
    Cards for the platform-wide surfaces: Messages, Looking for Group, War Stories, Timestamps, Homebrew (coming soon).

A setting filter at the top lets you scope the drill-down surfaces by setting. Your choice carries through.


MESSAGES
--------
Direct conversations with players, GMs, and visitors. Each thread shows a conversation history with timestamps. Click "+ New Message" to start a thread. Search bar filters by participant.

Your account avatar shows next to your username on every Campfire surface.


LOOKING FOR GROUP
-----------------
A bulletin board for finding GMs or players.

Post as either:
  - GM seeking players, or
  - Player seeking game.

Each post has a title, body, optional setting tag, and an optional schedule note. Other people click "I'm Interested" to express interest. As the post author, you see a roster of who's interested. You can DM them directly or, if you're a GM, send them a Story invite. You can also kick interested users from your post.

Share button pushes a post to Reddit, Twitter, Facebook, or copies a direct link.


FORUMS
------
Forums are being redesigned and not documented in this version. A revised Forums chapter will land when the redesign ships.


WAR STORIES
-----------
A cross-Story feed for session highlights, the time the party blew up the wrong building, the time someone rolled double-6 on a Recruitment Check.

  - Title and body.
  - Optional campaign tag.
  - Attach images and PDFs.
  - Edit and delete your own posts.

Survivor-authored posts enter a moderation queue. Admin-authored posts go live immediately.


TIMESTAMPS
----------
A utility tool. Pick a date, time, and timezone, get a Discord-compatible timestamp token (looks like `<t:1234567890:F>`). Paste it into Discord and it renders as the local time for every viewer.

Six format options plus raw Unix seconds. Useful for scheduling sessions across timezones.


HOMEBREW (COMING SOON)
----------------------
Placeholder for custom rules, house variants, fan content. Visible card but not yet open for posting.


SETTING HUBS - WHAT'S INSIDE
----------------------------
Click a hub card from the portal. The hub renders four sections:

  HEADER     Setting name, tagline, blurb, "Run a campaign in this setting" CTA.
  CANON      A timeline of canon pins for this setting, each with category icon and a "View on map" deep-link.
  COMMUNITIES  Published world communities sourced from any campaign tagged with this setting. Only approved ones render.
  FEED       Recent posts from Forums, War Stories, and LFG filtered to this setting.

The feed shows the setting carrying through from the portal's setting filter. Clicking through to a full surface preserves your setting choice.


COMMUNITY FEEDS
---------------
Each Community (Chapter 12) has its own activity feed surfaced through the Campfire layer. Open a community from My Communities, then click "Feed" to see community-scoped posts and post directly.
