====================================================================
 11. VEHICLES
====================================================================

A vehicle is a multi-seat token that the party drives between campaign-map pins, garages in tactical scenes, brews in, and fights from. Vehicles have fuel, cargo, slots for passengers, and a sheet of their own.


WHERE THEY LIVE
---------------
Open the Vehicle dropdown in the Table header to see the campaign's vehicles, or click the vehicle bubble that floats on the campaign map at the party's position.

The vehicle sheet opens in a popout window so you can keep it docked while you play.


SEAT ASSIGNMENT
---------------
A vehicle has named slots:

  DRIVER     Required to move. Rolls Drive checks for travel and combat.
  NAVIGATOR  The shotgun seat. Optional. Rolls NAVIGATE checks for routing, hazard-spotting, brewing.
  BREWER     Optional crafting station - see Brewing below.
  PASSENGERS One per remaining seat.

Click any empty slot in the popout to assign a player. A Close-range gate applies - the player has to be within 30 ft of the vehicle to climb in. Click MOVE HERE to walk a player into a slot in one action; the assignment confirms automatically when they arrive.

Click Disembark on any occupied slot to drop the player out. Their token lands next to the vehicle.

Passengers in motion vanish from the tactical map - they're inside the vehicle. A small count badge on the vehicle token shows how many people are aboard.


FUEL
----
Every vehicle has a fuel tank. Driving burns fuel; running out strands you.

Fuel storage works via INSTALLABLE 55-GALLON DRUMS. Each drum installed in a vehicle adds a fuel reserve. Drums are visible in the vehicle's cargo and can be transferred between vehicles, refilled at fuel pins on the world map, or scavenged.

The vehicle sheet shows current fuel as a bar with explicit gallons remaining.


BREWING
-------
Some vehicles support BREWING - a way to convert raw materials into alcohol, biodiesel, or other consumables while you drive. Requires a Brewer in the brewer slot and a Brewing Supplies stockpile.

GATHER MATERIALS is the in-vehicle action that converts cargo into brewing-supply units. The Brewer rolls a check; outcome determines yield.

Brewing fires its own narrative banner in the roll feed (BREW prefix) with six outcome variants.


CARGO
-----
Each vehicle has cargo capacity, expressed in slots or weight depending on vehicle type. Cargo is shared - any campaign member can edit a vehicle's inventory. Items can be moved between PC inventory and vehicle cargo freely while the PC is at the vehicle.


THE TACTICAL MAP
----------------
A vehicle in a tactical scene shows as a multi-cell object token. Driver + Navigator + Passenger positions are slot-anchored to the token. Open the vehicle popout from the token to see and reassign seats.

MOVE HERE on a tactical seat assignment auto-snaps the player's token to the seat cell.

Vehicles can take damage. A vehicle's WP and RP track like a character's - they have their own Lasting Wounds chip when seriously damaged.


COMBAT
------
The Driver can declare Vehicle Attack actions: ramming, swerving, drive-bys. Each fires its own narrative banner (DRIVE prefix). Navigators can declare NAVIGATE actions to spot hazards or set up the Driver's roll.

Passengers can take normal actions out the windows - shooting, throwing, etc. - within their weapon range from the vehicle.
