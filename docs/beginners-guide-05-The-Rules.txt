====================================================================
 5. THE RULES
====================================================================

Click "The Rules" in the sidebar. The complete Xero Sum Engine SRD lives in-app at /rules.

This is the canonical source for every rule in the game. When this guide and The Rules disagree, The Rules win.


EIGHT SECTIONS
--------------
  Overview               Introduction, key features, in-world time, naming conventions.
  Core Mechanics         The dice check, modifiers, attribute checks, Fill In The Gaps, First Impressions, Coordinated Effort, Insight Dice, Making the Case, Negotiations.
  Character Overview     RAPID, skills, secondary stats, fleshing out.
  Character Creation     Backstory generation, Paradigms, character evolution.
  Skills                 Full skill list, plus deep dives on Inspiration and Psychology.
  Equipment              Item condition, item traits, armor, rations, upkeep.
  Combat                 Combat rounds, damage, healing, range, incapacitation, infection, stress, weapon repair, bestiary.
  Communities            Structure, recruitment, morale, apprentices, CRB additions.


FOUR APPENDICES
---------------
  Skills          Quick-reference table.
  Equipment       Quick-reference table.
  Paradigms       All twelve archetypes with full loadouts.
  Tables          The canonical lookup tables (Motivation, Complication, Lasting Damage, etc.).


HOW TO USE IT
-------------
The Rules is structured like a hyperlinked reference manual, not a tutorial. Look up what you need:

  - Stuck on a combat action? /rules/combat
  - Need a Motivation roll? /rules/appendix-tables
  - Wondering how an item trait works? /rules/equipment/item-traits

Cross-references between sections are linked. Forthcoming sections show a "forthcoming" tag - the content is being written.

When in doubt, this is where to look first.
