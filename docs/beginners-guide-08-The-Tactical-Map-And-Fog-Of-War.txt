====================================================================
 8. THE TACTICAL MAP AND FOG OF WAR
====================================================================

The Tactical Map is a grid-based canvas for combat positioning. Fog of War is the layer of darkness that hides it until your players walk into the light.

A full standalone reference lives at docs/tactical-map-guide.txt. This chapter is the overview.


OPENING IT
----------
From the Table, click "Tactical Map" in the header to swap from the Campaign Map.

If your Story has no scenes yet, the Create Scene modal opens automatically. If scenes exist, the most recent one activates.


CREATING A SCENE
----------------
Click "Map Setup" in the header to open the scene controls in a separate window. New scenes start there.

  - Name (required).
  - Grid dimensions - 20 columns x 15 rows by default.
  - Cell size in feet - 3 ft per cell. This drives all range calculations.

A new scene auto-activates and broadcasts to anyone watching the Table.


THE GRID
--------
Cells use Chebyshev distance - the greater of the X and Y deltas. A diagonal step counts as 1, not 1.4.

Scene controls (in the Map Setup popout):
  Scene dropdown   switch scenes or create a new one.
  Background       upload a JPG or PNG. Scale with the corner handles.
  Cols / Rows      grid dimensions.
  Cell (ft) / (px) feet per cell, pixel size on screen.
  Grid color, opacity, ON/OFF.
  Lock / Unlock    locks the background image.
  Fit to Map       zooms to fit the full scene.
  Edit Fog         enters fog paint mode (see below).


TOKENS
------
Three kinds:

  PC TOKENS      Round, blue border. Owning player drags their own; GM can drag any.
  NPC TOKENS     Round, red border. GM-only drag. Hidden from players by default until combat starts.
  OBJECT TOKENS  Square, orange border. Multi-cell footprint for crates, vehicles, world objects.

Three special object types shape the battlefield:

  WALLS    Block movement and vision.
  DOORS    Block movement when closed. Block vision when closed; pass vision when open. Players click to toggle.
  WINDOWS  Block movement but pass vision. PCs see through glass.


RANGE AND MOVEMENT
------------------
Selecting a token brings up three range circles:

  ENGAGED (5 ft)   Green. Melee radius.
  MOVE (9 ft)      Blue. Move-action distance.
  WEAPON RANGE     Red. Weapon's reach.

The GM sees these around every selected token. Players see them only around their own PC.

Movement during combat costs 1 action and is bounded by the Move circle. Outside combat it's free.


PINGING
-------
Hold ALT and left-click any cell to drop a ping. GM pings orange; player pings green. Visible to everyone, fades in about a second.


FOG OF WAR
----------
A layer of darkness the GM paints over the tactical map. Players see fogged cells as solid black: no terrain, no tokens, no labels.

Each PC token clears 6 cells of fog around itself in every direction - a circle of light that travels with the player. The circle respects walls (block), closed doors (block), open doors (pass), and windows (pass).

ENTERING EDIT MODE
Click "Edit Fog" in the scene controls. Three tools and two bulk actions:

  PAINT      click and drag to fog cells one at a time.
  RECTANGLE  click and drag to fog every cell inside a rectangle.
  ERASE      click and drag to clear fog off cells.
  Fog All    fogs the entire scene.
  Clear All  clears all fog.

Click "Done" to exit edit mode.

Fog stays painted across reloads, reconnects, and session boundaries. It clears only when you erase it.


SHARING
-------
Players see the tactical map only when the GM has shared it. Combat auto-shares. Outside combat, click "Share Map" in the Table header.

Switching scenes auto-broadcasts the switch - players follow you through.
